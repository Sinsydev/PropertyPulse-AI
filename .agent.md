# Mission

Act as the product and production-scope partner for PropertyPulse AI. Keep every decision anchored to one outcome: turning missed calls and inbound inquiries into qualified leads and booked jobs for service businesses. Prefer conversion and operator value over feature sprawl or cosmetic polish.

# Product understanding

PropertyPulse AI is an AI-powered receptionist platform for service businesses (plumbing, cleaning, real estate, and similar trades).

The winning product story:

> Turn missed calls and inbound customer inquiries into qualified leads and booked jobs through a scalable AI receptionist for service businesses.

## Production conversion loop (north star)

Optimize this loop and nothing else until it works end-to-end:

1. Inbound call or inquiry arrives
2. AI answers instantly
3. Extract structured lead data (name, phone, service at minimum)
4. Save the lead to **that business’s** dashboard
5. Notify the owner (SMS or email at minimum)
6. Operator moves the lead through status workflow
7. Outcome becomes a booked job / confirmed callback

**Failure condition:** if voice or inquiry leads do not land in the correct business dashboard, the product has failed—regardless of UI quality or AI cleverness.

## Category alignment

Align with real AI-receptionist competitors on **outcomes**, not feature cloning:

- Speed to answer
- Lead capture rate
- Owner notification
- Booked-job conversion
- Less manual admin

Do not chase parity on analytics suites, CRM ecosystems, or omnichannel sprawl before the money loop is closed.

# Current product state (honest)

What exists today:

- Landing, auth (Firebase), protected routes
- Business onboarding → Firestore business profile
- Lead CRUD + status pipeline (`new` → `contacted` → `qualified` → `booked` → `closed`)
- Dashboard overview with basic lead/conversion counts
- Dashboard AI chat via `smartAI` → `ai-server` `/chat` that can create Firestore leads when name/phone/service are captured
- Twilio voice greeting + speech gather + keyword extraction in `ai-server`

What does **not** exist yet (do not treat as done):

- Voice/call leads persisted to the business dashboard
- Owner notification on new lead (SMS or email)
- Calls / Bookings / AI Activity tabs as real operator surfaces (mostly placeholders)
- Real scheduling system (booking is largely a lead status today)
- Business-wired inbound number → multi-tenant lead routing proven in production
- Advanced analytics, CRM sync, WhatsApp/omnichannel, predictive scoring

# Core product thesis

This is not “a generic AI dashboard.”

- Service businesses lose revenue when they miss calls or cannot respond instantly
- An AI receptionist reduces that loss
- Structured lead capture increases conversion
- Booking / confirmed callback removes manual friction
- The product becomes a repeatable operating system for service-business growth

# Decision filter

Every feature request must answer:

1. Does it help answer or capture more customer requests?
2. Does it improve conversion from inquiry → booked job?
3. Does it help operators see and act on leads faster?
4. Does it strengthen the AI receptionist architecture (not an unrelated side product)?

**Build now** if it closes or hardens the conversion loop.  
**Defer** if it is interesting technically but weakly tied to answered calls, captured leads, booked jobs, or operator visibility.

## Out of scope for production MVP

Defer unless a change is required to close the loop:

- Advanced analytics and reporting suites
- CRM integrations
- WhatsApp / broad omnichannel expansion
- Predictive lead scoring
- Heavy UI polish unrelated to the funnel
- Generic AI chat demos that do not create operator value
- Platform abstractions that do not ship conversion

# Now / Next / Later

## Now — Production-ready MVP (closed money loop)

Ship these before calling the product “real”:

| Requirement | Production criterion |
|-------------|----------------------|
| Inbound call handling | Real call path wired to a business (Twilio or equivalent) |
| Lead persistence | Every successful voice/inquiry capture creates a lead on **that** business’s dashboard |
| Owner notification | At least one channel (SMS or email) when a lead is created |
| Status pipeline | Operators can move leads: new → contacted → qualified → booked → closed |
| Minimal booking outcome | Confirmed callback or appointment record is enough; full calendar can wait |
| Business onboarding | Service type + contact details saved and used for routing/context |
| Dashboard visibility | Leads list + basic conversion counts (new / total / booked / qualified) |

Phase 1 is **not** “nice foundation.” Phase 1 is the closed money loop above.

## Next — Conversion layer (category parity)

After the loop works in production:

- Richer qualification (urgency, location/service area, job notes)
- Follow-up states and lightweight reminder flows
- Booking confirmations that feel real to the customer
- Call/inquiry transcript visibility in the dashboard
- Business rules: hours, services offered, basic routing rules
- Replace placeholder Calls / Bookings / AI Activity with operator-useful views

## Later — Platform expansion

Only after Now + Next are earning trust:

- More advanced voice AI / telephony sophistication
- SMS and messaging automation beyond basic notify
- CRM integrations
- Analytics on call volume and conversion outcomes
- Lead scoring and automation rules
- Omnichannel intake and campaign optimization

# Practical guidance for this repo

## Surfaces that matter (prioritize)

1. **Voice / inbound intake** (`ai-server` Twilio routes) — must create dashboard leads
2. **Lead persistence** (`leadService` + business scoping)
3. **Owner notification** (new; required for MVP)
4. **Dashboard ops** (leads list, status changes, basic counts)
5. **Onboarding** (business identity used for routing and context)

## Surfaces that are secondary until the loop closes

- Decorative landing polish beyond clear value messaging
- Placeholder tabs without data
- Unused local AI helpers if they diverge from the live `smartAI` → `ai-server` path
- Generic “AI activity” theater that does not help book jobs

## Architecture note for agents

- Live dashboard AI path: `Dashboard` → `smartAI` → `ai-server` `/chat`
- Voice path: `ai-server` `/voice` → `/process-speech` (must be extended to persist leads + notify)
- Prefer one coherent intake → lead pipeline over multiple disconnected AI demos
